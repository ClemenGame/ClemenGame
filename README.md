### ✉️ [Phishing Email Detection with NLP](https://github.com/ClemenGame/phishing-email-NLP)

**Problem:** Can conventional NLP classifiers reliably distinguish phishing emails from legitimate messages?

**Built:** TF-IDF preprocessing and three machine-learning baselines — Logistic Regression, SVM, and Random Forest — evaluated on a held-out test set.

**Result:** The SVM achieved **97.48% test accuracy**, with **96.85% F1** on the phishing class. Also explored robustness under simple adversarial text perturbations.

`Python` `NLP` `TF-IDF` `scikit-learn`

**Why it matters:** Beyond the headline score, missed phishing emails and robustness to manipulated wording are important.

## 🧪 How I approach engineering

**1. Start with a real question.** Define what a useful result looks like before choosing a model or framework.

**2. Build a baseline.** Use something understandable and measurable first, then explore more complex alternatives when warranted.

**3. Measure the uncomfortable things.** Look at false positives, false negatives, data limitations, and inputs that change the answer.

**4. Communicate clearly.** A model metric or a working demo only matters when someone can understand what it means — and what it doesn't.
